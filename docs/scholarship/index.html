What a strong data study includes

The turbolapper method is the public path for that study. A strong study has five parts.

  1. A question a reader can restate

    One sentence is enough. The question names the decision, the place, and the year.

  2. Data with a named source

    Record the source, the geography, and the year. Public series are enough. The US county economics outline in this repository is one ready corpus, built from BEA, IRS, and Census Bureau tables.

  3. Local analysis on a Mac in turbolapper

    Prep notes name the columns, the joins, and the documented views. turbolapper AFM and turbolapper Medium both keep the file on the Mac. Starter is free, with a local data cap of 100 MB on AFM and 20 MB on Medium and Heavy. Other tiers are on the turbolapper pricing page.

  4. Findings a reader can check

    turbolapper labels confidence Verified, Estimated, or Directional. Each claim is meant to show its work. Where a series is missing, say so.

  5. A published write-up

    turbolapper turns the work into a slide deck. Cite the sources and the year, and list the questions the data leaves open.

These five parts describe the method. Details and deadline: see turbolapper.com.

How to apply

Build the study in turbolapper and publish it where a reader can open it. Get the app on the turbolapper download page. The example study outline shows questions and sources for a county study, and leaves the findings for your run.

Details and deadline: see turbolapper.com.
